Recipe #1

  • Time:35 min
  • Serves: 1 person(s)
Ingredients Quantity
8oz (Grass Fed) Filet Mignon #1 Quantity #1

Step 1: Take filet mignon out of refrigdegrator to rest for 10- 15 minutes (This will bring the steak closer to room temperature to ensure it cooks evenly on the stove top).

Step 1: Prepare oil in your pan and set stove top notch to levels 3-4. See time instructions for specific cooks of steak here.

Step 2: Cover all sides of the steak in salt and black pepper (Later when cooking, this layer will form a crisp and crunchy crust).

Step 3: Add your steak to the pan making sure to press it into the pan.

Step 4: Cook steak until there is a solid brown crust formed on each side.

Step 5: Take steak off the pan and set it to the side.

Step 6: In your pan add 3-4 tbls of unsalted butter, garlic, thyme, rosemary, and other herbs of your liking.

Step 7: Once the butter has melted and begins to brown, add your steak back into the pan and use a spoon to scoop the butter over your steak. This is called "basting".
